# Market Research

Structured competitive and market analysis with source attribution.

## Steps
1. **Define market** — identify the target market segment and geography
2. **Map competitors** — find direct and indirect competitors, their positioning
3. **Analyse offerings** — features, pricing, strengths, weaknesses per competitor
4. **Size the market** — TAM/SAM/SOM estimates with methodology and sources
5. **Identify trends** — emerging patterns, threats, and opportunities
6. **Compile report** — structured findings with source attribution

## Output Format
- **Market overview** — segment definition, size estimates
- **Competitor matrix** — feature/price comparison table
- **SWOT per competitor**
- **Trend analysis** — 3-5 key trends with supporting evidence
- **Strategic recommendations**

## Rules
- Cite every data point with its source
- Distinguish between estimates and verified figures
- Note the date of any market data (markets change fast)
