# Article Writing

Write high-quality long-form content with consistent voice and structure.

## Steps
1. **Brief** — confirm topic, audience, tone, target length, and key points
2. **Research** — gather supporting evidence, data, and quotes
3. **Outline** — create a structured outline with section headers
4. **Draft** — write the full article following the outline
5. **Polish** — self-edit for clarity, flow, and anti-slop (see rules)
6. **Deliver** — output the final article in markdown

## Anti-Slop Rules
- No "In today's fast-paced world" or similar filler openings
- No "It's important to note that" — just state the thing
- No "Let's dive in" or "Without further ado"
- No "In conclusion" — the conclusion should be obvious from context
- Avoid passive voice unless deliberately chosen for emphasis
- Cut any sentence that restates the previous one in different words
- Specific > generic: use real numbers, names, and examples

## Output Format
- Title
- Subtitle/hook
- Body sections with headers
- Call to action (if applicable)
